Dynamic
Constitutive
Modeling
Core Research

A crash or impact simulation is only as good as its flow stress model. I determine that model from discrete dynamic test data, using SVD/CP tensor decomposition to test whether a decoupled (Johnson–Cook-type) form is admissible and to extrapolate beyond the calibrated strain-rate range (TEDI-FS). Scope: isotropic metals under J2 plasticity.

Battery Impact
Safety & Health
Management
Current Focus

Lithium-ion cells can carry latent impact damage that shows no external sign. This stream characterizes failure modes and energy thresholds under impact, detects damage in real time from acoustic emission with deep learning, and links mechanical damage to electrochemical degradation with multiphysics FEM.
