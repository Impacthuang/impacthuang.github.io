News & Highlights

Recent research updates, publications, and academic activities.

Aug 2026
🎉
Paper Accepted in International Journal of Impact Engineering — Fourth First-Authored IJIE Paper
"Tensor-decomposition-based inverse characterization of flow stress for very-high strain-rate impact" (First Author, with Q.M. Li) has been accepted. The TEDI-FS framework extrapolates SHPB-calibrated flow stress models beyond their calibrated strain-rate domain under consistency constraints, and validates the extrapolation against Taylor-Hopkinson impact tests up to ~105 s-1. This completes a four-paper IJIE series (2023–2026) on data-driven flow stress modeling, from verifying decoupled equations to validated extrapolation at very-high strain rates.
