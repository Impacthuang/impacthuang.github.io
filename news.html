Sep 2026
Paper Published in Journal of Materials Engineering and Performance
"A Data-Driven Decoupled Hardening Model: Uncovering the Fundamental Mechanism of Non-uniform Bulging in Thin Sheets" (Corresponding Author, with Yunfei Deng, Weiyu Pu, and Xiaoyue Yang). A machine-learning-assisted non-associated Hill48 model in which anisotropic parameters evolve continuously with accumulated plastic strain, validated against Erichsen cupping tests on Al5083-H112 sheets and implemented as an ABAQUS/Explicit VUMAT. Identifies strain-dependent R-value evolution as the primary driver of non-uniform bulging in sheet forming — turning the "anisotropy of metals" direction from in-progress work into a first result.

Aug 2026
Paper Published in International Journal of Impact Engineering — Fourth First-Authored IJIE Paper
"Tensor-decomposition-based inverse characterization of flow stress for very-high strain-rate impact" (First Author, with Q.M. Li) has been published. The TEDI-FS framework extrapolates SHPB-calibrated flow stress models beyond their calibrated strain-rate domain under consistency constraints, and validates the extrapolation against Taylor-Hopkinson impact tests up to ~105 s-1. This completes a four-paper IJIE series (2023–2026) on data-driven flow stress modeling, from verifying decoupled equations to validated extrapolation at very-high strain rates.
